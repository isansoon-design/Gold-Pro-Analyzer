Privacy Policy

Gold Pro Analyzer · Last updated: 9 August 2026

Gold Pro Analyzer (“we”, “the app”) is an educational market-structure tool. This policy explains what data we handle.

Data we process

  • On your device: profile fields you enter (name, email, mobile), favorites, educational reports inbox, theme, and consent flags. Stored in on-device storage.
  • Optional notifications: if you allow push, a device token may be registered with our notification provider (e.g. Firebase) to deliver educational alerts you opted into.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous technical logs may be used to fix crashes and improve stability.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not provide brokerage or execute trades.
  • We do not require an account to study charts in the basic educational experience.

Your choices

  • You can delete on-device data from Profile → Delete all data on this device.
  • You can disable notifications in system settings.
  • Contact: support@goldproanalyzer.com

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over only.

International users

We aim to follow applicable privacy principles including UK GDPR-style transparency for users in relevant regions.

This page is provided for App Store and product transparency. Educational use only — not financial advice.
